Most small businesses start thinking about Black Friday in mid-November. By then, the best deals are already in your customers' inboxes from every major retailer on the planet — and you're scrambling to compete with last-minute discounts instead of a real strategy.

The businesses that win BFCM — Black Friday and Cyber Monday — start now. Ten weeks out is the sweet spot: enough time to build your offer, warm your email list, schedule your campaigns, and set up automation that works while you're busy running your business on the busiest shopping weekend of the year.

This playbook gives you a concrete week-by-week plan. Whether you run a boutique, a service company, a med spa, or any other local business, there's a BFCM strategy that works for you.

## Why Black Friday Still Matters for Small Businesses

You might wonder if BFCM is really for small businesses — or just Amazon and big-box retailers. The data says otherwise.

**Shoppers actively seek out local and small business alternatives.** Small Business Saturday (the day after Black Friday) generated over $17 billion in consumer spending in 2024, and a growing segment of shoppers explicitly choose to "shop small" as a values-driven decision.

**Service businesses have a massive opportunity.** Most BFCM marketing advice focuses on physical products, but service businesses — spas, fitness studios, consultants, home services — can sell gift cards, prepaid packages, and memberships at BFCM prices. There is no inventory risk, no shipping deadline, and margins are often better than product-based businesses.

**Email and SMS over-deliver during BFCM.** Open rates for BFCM emails run 20–30% higher than annual averages as shoppers are actively in purchase mode. The inbox is crowded, but so is consumer buying intent — and your list is your most valuable asset during this period.

**The window is expanding.** "Black Friday" now effectively runs from the first week of November through Cyber Monday. That's four weeks of elevated consumer spending, not a single day.

## The 10-Week BFCM Countdown

![Infographic showing a 10-week BFCM marketing countdown in four phases: Strategy and Offers, List Building, Pre-Launch, and Launch Week](https://vwy1t1uzxwusskun.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/black-friday-cyber-monday-small-business-guide/inline-1.png)

Think of your BFCM preparation in four phases:

### Phase 1: Strategy and Offer (Weeks 1–3, Now through Early October)

This is the planning phase. No campaigns yet — just decisions.

**Define your BFCM offer.** The most common mistake: discounting everything and eroding your margins without a clear strategy. Instead, pick one or two featured offers and build your campaign around them.

**Choose your channels.** Which will drive your BFCM: email, SMS, Instagram, paid ads, in-store events, or some combination? Be realistic about what you can execute well with your resources.

**Set your revenue goal.** A target gives your campaign direction. Whether it's "sell 50 gift cards" or "book 30 BFCM appointments," a concrete number helps you measure success and scale effort appropriately.

**Build your landing page or offer page.** Create a simple, focused page for your BFCM deal. This becomes the destination for all your email, SMS, and social traffic. If you use a booking system or e-commerce platform, set up the promo code or package now so it's ready when you launch.

### Phase 2: List Building and Content Prep (Weeks 4–6, Early to Late October)

The size and warmth of your email and SMS list on November 1st directly determines how much revenue your BFCM campaign generates. This phase is about filling both.

**Run a pre-BFCM lead magnet.** Offer something valuable in exchange for an email address and explicit opt-in for your upcoming deals. Examples:

- "Join our VIP list for early access to our Black Friday offers — exclusive to subscribers"
- "Download our [your niche] holiday gift guide" (and capture email on the download)
- "Enter our fall giveaway" (with email + SMS opt-in)

**Warm your existing list.** If your email list has gone quiet since summer, send two to three re-engagement emails in October. Content ideas: fall seasonal tips, a behind-the-scenes story, a customer success story, a "what we've been working on" update. The goal is inbox familiarity before your BFCM pitch arrives.

**Prepare your content in batches.** Write all your BFCM emails, SMS messages, and social captions now — before November gets hectic. Schedule what you can. Batch the rest into a content folder you can post from without scrambling.

### Phase 3: Pre-Launch Warmup (Weeks 7–9, November 1–20)

The month of November before Black Friday is your runway. Use it.

**"Deals dropping soon" tease.** Starting November 1, begin telegraphing your BFCM offer without revealing it. "Something big is coming on November 28th — make sure you're on our list." This creates anticipation and urgency to stay subscribed.

**VIP early access.** If you have a loyalty program, membership tier, or simply a segment of your most loyal customers, offer them early access to your BFCM deal. November 22–26 (the week before Black Friday) is a natural window for this. Loyal customers feel special; they buy more and tell others.

**Paid ads warmup.** If you're running Meta or Google ads for BFCM, start running low-budget awareness and retargeting ads now. Building pixel audiences before your campaign launches dramatically reduces your cost-per-conversion during the actual launch week.

### Phase 4: Launch and Cyber Monday (Week 10, November 27–December 1)

This is execution week. Your strategy is set, your content is ready, your list is warm — now you execute.

**Black Friday (Nov 27):** Send your main BFCM offer at 6–8 AM. Follow up with a same-day reminder to non-openers around 2–4 PM. Post on social. Text your SMS list.

**Small Business Saturday (Nov 28):** If you have a physical location, lean into this. Run an in-store event, partner with a neighboring business, and post behind-the-scenes content throughout the day.

**Cyber Monday (Dec 1):** Pivot to a digital-only offer or a "last chance" push on your main deal. Cyber Monday is when service businesses and digital offers shine — gift cards, online booking packages, digital products.

**Urgency close:** Send a "24 hours left" or "last chance" email/SMS on Monday evening before your offer expires. Urgency emails consistently drive 15–25% of total BFCM revenue for email-driven campaigns.

## Crafting Your BFCM Offer

The offer is the most important decision you'll make. Here's a framework for service and local businesses:

### The Three Offer Types

**Discount offer:** Straightforward percentage or dollar discount on your most popular product or service. Works well but trains customers to wait for sales. Best when used selectively on new customer acquisition.

- Example: 25% off your first facial or massage appointment, booked during BFCM

**Bundle offer:** Combine multiple products or services into a package at a compelling total price. Margin-friendly because the perceived value is high even if your cost is low.

- Example: Med spa "Holiday Glow Package" — three services bundled at a price that's 20% less than buying separately

**Gift card / prepaid offer:** Sell gift cards or prepaid sessions at a discount. You collect cash now, deliver the service later. Zero inventory risk, excellent cash flow, and gift cards often go unused (adding to your margin).

- Example: Buy a $200 gift card for $160 — ideal for holiday gifting

### Industry-Specific Offer Ideas

**Med spas and aesthetic practices:** Holiday treatment bundles (Botox + filler + one facial), VIP membership launch at BFCM pricing, treatment package series at 20% off, gift cards at 15–20% discount. See our [med spa seasonal promotions guide](/blog/seasonal-promotions-aesthetic-practices-guide) for more offer frameworks.

**Home services:** Prepaid maintenance packages (e.g., 3 HVAC tune-ups for the price of 2), priority scheduling add-ons, gift cards for holiday gifting (home cleaning, lawn care, pest control). Year-end contracts for commercial customers.

**Fitness and wellness studios:** Founding member pricing for new membership tiers, class pack deals (20 classes for the price of 16), referral-powered BFCM deals (you get a discount, they get a discount), apparel and merchandise bundles.

**Restaurants and food businesses:** Holiday catering packages booked during BFCM with a deposit, premium gift cards, loyalty point multiplier events, private dining reservation releases.

## Email and SMS Campaigns That Convert

![Small business owner composing a Black Friday email campaign on a laptop decorated with autumn accessories, with an SMS campaign notification on their smartphone](https://vwy1t1uzxwusskun.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/black-friday-cyber-monday-small-business-guide/inline-2.png)

Your email list is your most valuable BFCM asset. Here's the sequence that works:

### The 5-Email BFCM Sequence

**Email 1 — Tease (November 18–21):**
Subject line: "Something's coming. Mark your calendar."
One paragraph teasing the offer, one CTA to make sure they're watching their inbox. No deal revealed yet.

**Email 2 — VIP Early Access (November 25–26):**
Subject line: "You're getting early access 🎁"
Reveal the full offer, 24–48 hours before public launch. Exclusive early-access link. Creates a loyalty payoff.

**Email 3 — Black Friday Launch (November 27, 6–8 AM):**
Subject line: "Black Friday is here — [offer details]"
Full offer, clear CTA, deadline stated. One image. Keep copy tight — shoppers skim hard on BFCM day.

**Email 4 — Last-call reminder (November 27, 2–4 PM, non-openers only):**
Subject line: "Still available: [offer] — ends tonight"
Send to everyone who didn't open the morning email. Personalized subject line lift applies here.

**Email 5 — Cyber Monday close (December 1):**
Subject line: "Last chance: BFCM deal ends tonight"
Final urgency push. Mention it's the last opportunity until next year. Add a countdown or deadline timestamp.

### SMS Campaign Structure

Text messages outperform email on open rate (98% vs ~25%) but require permission and brevity.

**SMS 1 — Day-before teaser (November 26):**
"[Business name]: Tomorrow's the day 🛍️ Our biggest sale of the year drops at 8am. Watch for it! Reply STOP to opt out."

**SMS 2 — Launch (November 27, 8 AM):**
"[Business name] Black Friday is LIVE: [offer summary]. Book/shop here: [link]. Limited spots. Ends Sunday."

**SMS 3 — Cyber Monday close (December 1, 10 AM):**
"Last chance! [offer] expires TONIGHT at midnight. [link] — [Business name]"

## Social Media Strategy for BFCM

Social media during BFCM isn't about organic reach — it's about creating social proof, building anticipation, and retargeting people who've already visited your offer page or profile.

**November 1–15:** Post "counting down to something big" content. Mix in customer testimonials, behind-the-scenes prep, and seasonal content. Start running low-budget awareness ads if applicable.

**November 16–25:** Tease the offer more directly. Show packaging, the deal structure, or customer reactions from previous BFCM offers. Build anticipation with "subscribers get early access" messaging.

**November 27–28:** Go all-in on promotion. Stories throughout the day, Reels of in-store activity, live counts of units/spots remaining. Real-time urgency converts.

**December 1:** Final push. "Last day, last chance" content. Customer reviews from BFCM weekend.

## Automation: Let Your Tech Do the Heavy Lifting

The businesses that scale BFCM aren't doing it manually. Here's the automation stack that makes a difference:

**Abandoned inquiry follow-up.** When someone visits your booking page or offer landing page but doesn't complete a purchase, a well-timed follow-up sequence recovers a significant portion of that traffic. Set up an email automation triggered by page visit + no conversion, sending two to three follow-ups over 48 hours.

**Post-purchase upsell.** When someone claims your BFCM offer, they're in a buying mindset. Trigger an immediate follow-up email or confirmation page that offers a relevant add-on at a small discount. "Add a [complementary service] for just $X today."

**Review request sequence.** After customers use their BFCM purchase (typically in December or January), trigger an automated review request. BFCM converts new customers — reviews from those customers build social proof for the next 12 months.

**Gift card reminders.** Gift cards sold during BFCM often go unredeemed. Set up a "your gift card is waiting" reminder sequence that fires 60, 90, and 120 days after purchase. This converts stored value into actual service bookings.

## Local Business BFCM Tactics That Stand Out

![Excited shoppers entering a warmly lit local boutique decorated for Black Friday, with festive window displays and happy customers browsing inside](https://vwy1t1uzxwusskun.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/black-friday-cyber-monday-small-business-guide/inline-3.png)

If you have a physical location, you have assets that online-only businesses don't: physical space, human interaction, and the ability to create memorable experiences.

**Host a Black Friday event.** Turn your store or studio into a destination, not just a transaction. Add something experiential: a complimentary service preview, a live demo, a champagne toast for your best customers, a local artist. Experiences create social content — your customers become your marketing.

**Partner with neighboring businesses.** A "shop the block" partnership across three to five local businesses drives traffic that benefits everyone. Cross-promote on social, share email lists with permission, offer a bundle deal that spans multiple businesses. For more on local partnerships, see our [local partnership marketing guide](/blog/local-partnership-marketing-guide).

**Geo-targeted digital ads.** Run Meta ads targeting a 5–10 mile radius around your location starting November 21. Focus on retargeting people who have visited your website or engaged with your social profiles. A $15–25/day budget during BFCM week can drive meaningful foot traffic and bookings.

**In-store urgency mechanics.** Countdown clocks on your website and in-store. "Only 12 spots remaining" messaging. Real-time social posts showing demand. Physical "sold" or "limited" tags on offer signage. Urgency at the point of sale is one of the highest-leverage conversion tactics available to local businesses.

**Small Business Saturday activation.** November 28 is explicitly positioned as "shop small" day. Lean into it fully — post "we're a local business" content, share your story, partner with American Express (they sometimes run small business promotions), and create an in-store experience specifically designed for that day.

## Measuring Your BFCM Performance

Track these eight metrics to understand what worked and what to improve next year:

| Metric                                | What It Tells You                   | Target                   |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Email open rate**                   | List engagement during BFCM         | 25–35%                   |
| **Email click-to-open rate**          | Offer relevance                     | 15–25%                   |
| **SMS click rate**                    | Urgency effectiveness               | 10–20%                   |
| **Offer redemption rate**             | Conversion from interested to buyer | Varies by offer          |
| **Revenue per subscriber**            | Email list value                    | Track vs. prior year     |
| **New vs. returning customers**       | Customer acquisition effectiveness  | 30–50% new               |
| **Average order / transaction value** | Upsell and bundle performance       | Track vs. annual average |
| **Gift card redemption rate**         | Post-BFCM follow-through            | 60–80% within 90 days    |

Review all eight metrics in the first week of December and document them. Your BFCM 2027 strategy starts with what you learned from 2026.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Black Friday worth it for service businesses that don't sell products?

Absolutely. Service businesses often perform better at BFCM than product businesses because their margins are higher, there's no inventory risk, and gift cards / prepaid packages are genuinely desirable holiday gifts. The key is packaging your offer as a gift or experience, not just a discount.

### How early is too early to start BFCM marketing?

Late October is the earliest most audiences accept BFCM messaging without feeling bombarded. The pre-campaign work — building your list, warming your audience, setting up automation — happens before that and is invisible to customers. What customers see should start around November 1, with a soft tease.

### Should I discount my highest-margin services?

Not necessarily. Bundle offers and gift card discounts protect margin better than straight discounts on your most profitable services. Consider discounting on lower-margin but high-volume offers to drive volume, and bundle your premium services to maintain margin while increasing perceived value.

### How do I compete with big retailers on BFCM?

You don't compete with them — you offer what they can't. A local business's advantages are relationships, personalization, local expertise, community connection, and in-person experience. Lean into those. Your "support local" messaging is a genuine differentiator. Customers who want Amazon deals will go to Amazon. Your job is to capture customers who want something better.

### What if I don't have an email list yet?

Start building it now. You have 10 weeks — enough time to build a meaningful list through a lead magnet, social media, existing customers, and in-store sign-up cards. Even 200–300 engaged subscribers can drive significant BFCM revenue for a local service business. An imperfect list built now outperforms a perfect list you haven't started yet.
