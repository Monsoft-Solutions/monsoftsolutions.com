October through December is the most valuable quarter of the year for most med spas—and the practices that plan for it now (in September) are the ones that fill their books months in advance, run their best gift card programs, and book January before the holidays even hit.

The practices that don't plan? They scramble in November, run generic discounts, and miss the window entirely.

This is your October-December playbook: what to promote, when to promote it, how to package it, and how to use email, SMS, and social to make Q4 your highest-revenue quarter yet.

## Why Q4 Is Different for Med Spas

The holiday quarter works differently than any other time of year because patient behavior shifts in three important ways.

**The gift-giving mindset.** From mid-October through Christmas, people are actively looking for gifts. A med spa gift card is a premium, aspirational gift—the kind of thing people want but won't always buy for themselves. That creates a completely different buyer: not just your existing patients, but their spouses, adult children, friends, and coworkers who are looking for something meaningful to give.

**Party prep demand.** Holiday parties, family gatherings, New Year's Eve events, and work functions create urgent, deadline-driven demand for injectables, facials, and skin treatments. Patients who might wait on Botox in June won't wait in November when they have a party in three weeks.

**Year-end benefits spending.** Patients with FSA (Flexible Spending Account) dollars must spend them before December 31 or lose them. This creates a predictable surge of patients motivated to use funds before the deadline—and they're often looking for elective aesthetic treatments that qualify.

Build your Q4 strategy around all three.

## The Four Holiday Patient Segments to Market To

Before you build campaigns, know who you're targeting. Q4 brings four distinct groups to your door, each with different motivations.

**1. Gift Card Buyers.** These are people shopping for someone else—often patients' spouses, adult children, or close friends. They want a premium, easy-to-gift option. They're not necessarily familiar with your specific services; they want confidence that you're trustworthy and that the gift is impressive.

**2. Self-Treating Patients.** Your existing patient base, treating themselves to something special before the holidays. They already trust you, they know your work, and they have a specific treatment in mind. Retention-focused offers and loyalty rewards work best here.

**3. Party Prep Patients.** New and existing patients with a specific deadline in mind—a holiday party, a family reunion, a New Year's Eve event. Urgency is their primary motivator. Clear availability windows and booking deadlines convert this segment.

**4. FSA/Year-End Benefits Spenders.** Patients with expiring FSA funds or year-end insurance situations. They're price-motivated not by discounts, but by the logic of "spend it before I lose it." A single email in November and December specifically addressing FSA spending can generate significant revenue.

## Month-by-Month Q4 Playbook

### October: Plant the Seeds

October is when you set up everything that pays off in November and December. Most practices wait until November to start thinking about holiday marketing. That's too late.

**What to do in October:**

**Launch your gift card program early.** Have physical and digital gift cards ready by October 1. October is when your most organized patients start thinking about holiday shopping. Don't make them wait.

**Run a fall skin refresh campaign.** Summer sun damage is real, and October is when patients start noticing it. Campaigns around chemical peels, laser skin resurfacing, IPL photofacials, and facials perform strongly in fall because patients have visible concerns (sun spots, texture, uneven tone) and the timing is right—less UV exposure means safer treatment windows for many procedures.

**Introduce your holiday packages.** October is the right time to introduce bundled treatment packages with a holiday angle—"The Holiday Glow Package," "The New You Bundle," or simply curated combinations of your most popular treatments. Packages simplify the buying decision and increase average transaction value.

**Start collecting holiday wish lists.** A simple insert at checkout or a link in your email newsletter asking "What's on your holiday wish list?" creates warm lead data. You can share this directly with patients' purchase decision-makers—"forward this to someone who wants to spoil you"—and it feels personal rather than salesy.

**Halloween tie-in (optional).** A "Boo-tiful Skin" promotion or "Scare Away Fine Lines" Botox special might feel gimmicky to some practices, but it works well on social media and can generate engagement. Keep it light and on-brand.

### November: The Revenue Surge

November is your biggest month of the quarter. Party prep demand peaks, gift card buying accelerates, and FSA conversations start.

**What to do in November:**

**Launch your Black Friday / Black Friday week promotion.** Black Friday has become a legitimate med spa marketing event. Patients expect deals on the Friday after Thanksgiving. This does not have to mean discounting your treatments—it can mean:

- Bonus gift card value ("Buy a $500 gift card, get a $75 treatment credit")
- Package deals exclusive to Black Friday week
- Complimentary add-ons for bookings made that week
- Priority booking access for your loyalty members

Run this as a multi-day event (Monday through Saturday the week of Thanksgiving, not just Friday) to extend the window and reduce booking bottlenecks.

**Party prep timeline content.** Publish a post and email campaign around "Holiday party in X weeks? Here's what to book now." Injectables typically require 2 weeks to fully settle. Chemical peels need 5–7 days of downtime. Laser resurfacing needs 1–2 weeks. Create a reverse timeline that tells patients exactly when to book each treatment before key holiday dates.

**The FSA alert email.** Send a dedicated email in mid-November: "Don't lose your FSA dollars—use them on treatments you love." Be specific about which of your services are FSA/HSA eligible (typically: medical-grade facials billed under certain codes, some laser procedures, and consultations may qualify depending on your billing structure—confirm with your practice manager). This email consistently performs in the top 5% for open and click rates at this time of year.

**Gift card push.** Make gift cards impossible to miss throughout November. Feature them in:

- Every email (footer link + at least one dedicated campaign)
- Instagram and Facebook stories daily in the two weeks before Thanksgiving
- A front-desk display with elegant packaging
- Your booking confirmation emails ("Know someone who would love this? Gift cards available →")

### December: Urgency and Last-Minute Wins

December is shorter than it feels. The booking window closes fast, and by December 20, most patients aren't booking new appointments—they're wrapping gifts and traveling.

**What to do in December:**

**Create visible urgency.** Communicate booking availability clearly: "We have 14 appointment slots open before December 22—book yours now." Scarcity messaging works because it's true—you do have limited slots, and patients need to know before they're gone.

**The last-minute gift card email.** Send a dedicated email campaign around December 15 and December 20: "Still looking for the perfect gift? Our digital gift cards are available instantly." Digital gift cards solve the "I forgot to shop" problem elegantly, and a 12 PM email on December 23 can still convert.

**New Year's booking campaign.** Start booking January in December. New Year's resolutions about self-care, skin health, weight management, and aesthetic improvements are powerful motivators. Run campaigns in mid-to-late December:

- "Start 2027 looking and feeling your best—book your January consultation today"
- A New Year's treatment package at a special rate for bookings made in December
- A limited-time offer for your loyalty patients to pre-book their Q1 treatments before the calendar opens

This turns December into not just a revenue month but a pipeline-builder for January, which is historically slow for practices that don't plan for it.

**Holiday hours and coverage.** Communicate your holiday schedule clearly—in emails, on your website, on Google Business Profile, and at the front desk. Patients who can't reach you between December 23–26 will book someone else by the time you're back. If you're closed, enable your AI phone agent or chatbot to take bookings and answer FAQs.

## Building a Med Spa Gift Card Program That Actually Converts

Gift cards are Q4's highest-margin product—you receive payment immediately for future services that may never be redeemed (revenue recognition varies), and redeemed gift cards often generate additional spend beyond the card value.

The difference between a gift card program that generates $5,000 and one that generates $50,000 isn't the product—it's the presentation and promotion.

**Physical vs. digital.** Offer both. Physical gift cards with elegant packaging serve the in-person gifting experience and look premium on your front desk display. Digital gift cards (delivered by email) capture last-minute buyers and out-of-town gift-givers. Most practice management systems (Zenoti, Boulevard, Mindbody, Jane App) support both.

**The right denominations.** Offer gift cards at multiple price points—$100, $200, $300, $500, and $1,000. Many practices see their $300 cards as the sweet spot (feels generous, covers a real treatment), but always have the $500 and $1,000 options available for patients who want to give something exceptional. Don't cap at $200 out of fear of seeming too expensive.

**Bonus value promotions.** The most effective gift card promotion isn't a discount—it's bonus value. "Buy a $500 gift card, receive a complimentary $75 Hydrafacial" or "Purchase any gift card over $300, get 10% added to the value" creates urgency without cheapening your services. The recipient perceives full value, and you've maintained price integrity.

**Gift card presentation.** The physical experience matters. Invest in elegant packaging—a simple gift box, tissue paper, and a ribbon dramatically increase the perceived value. A handwritten card slot and a brief treatment menu tucked inside gives the recipient something to browse. Presentation converts browsers into buyers at the front desk.

**Train your team.** Your front desk staff should mention gift cards at every checkout from October through December. Not aggressively—a simple "By the way, if you're looking for a great holiday gift, we have beautiful gift cards available" closes a surprising number of add-on purchases from patients who hadn't thought of it.

## Holiday Email and SMS Campaign Framework

Don't improvise your messaging in November. Map out your campaign calendar in September so execution is clean when the season hits.

**Email sequence overview:**

- **October 1:** Gift card launch + fall skin refresh campaign
- **October 15:** Fall treatment spotlight (chemical peels, IPL, laser skin resurfacing)
- **October 31:** Halloween tie-in (optional) + early November booking reminder
- **November 5:** Holiday party prep timeline — "book before X date for [treatment]"
- **November 12:** Black Friday preview — "something special coming for our patients"
- **November 25–28:** Black Friday week promotion (send daily or every other day)
- **November 18:** FSA alert email — "use it before you lose it"
- **December 2:** December availability + gift card push
- **December 8:** "Book before December 22" urgency + party prep final call
- **December 15:** Gift card gift guide for last-minute shoppers
- **December 20:** Digital gift card last-minute push
- **December 26:** New Year's campaign launch — January booking offer

**SMS sequences:**

Use SMS for high-urgency messages only—flash sales, last-call booking reminders, and day-of offers. SMS for every campaign will train patients to ignore your texts.

- Black Friday offer launch (text on the morning of Black Friday)
- "Last 5 appointments before December 22" urgency push
- New Year's offer launch (December 26 or 27)

**HIPAA reminder:** Use only HIPAA-compliant platforms for patient communications (GoHighLevel, Klara, Weave, Podium with BAA). Do not reference specific procedures in texts to patients who haven't consented to that level of communication detail.

## Social Media Content for Q4

Social media in Q4 has a different cadence than the rest of the year. Here's what performs:

**Gift card content.** Lifestyle-oriented posts showing your gift cards in context—wrapped beautifully, displayed on a festive table, held by a smiling staff member. Content that makes followers imagine giving or receiving the gift converts better than product-focused posts.

**Treatment-specific booking countdown posts.** "6 weeks until Christmas — perfect timing for [treatment]" or "Only [X] slots left before the holidays" create urgency without being pushy.

**Before-and-after content (HIPAA-compliant).** Q4 is a great time to resurface your best patient results, with full consent documentation. Patients researching holiday treatments make buying decisions based on results.

**Behind-the-scenes holiday content.** Decorating the spa, team holiday prep, staff gift exchange. This humanizes your practice at a time when patients are in a warm, connection-oriented headspace. It builds trust and makes your team relatable.

**Patient appreciation content.** Thanksgiving week is the perfect time to express genuine gratitude to your patient community. A simple "Thank you for trusting us with your care this year" post builds enormous goodwill and reminds patients of their relationship with your practice.

## Setting Up January Before December Ends

The practices that have a strong January aren't lucky—they booked it in December.

January is notoriously slow in aesthetics without intentional planning. But the patient mindset in late December is uniquely receptive to New Year's messaging. Leverage it.

**New Year campaigns to build:**

- **"New Year, New You" consultation special.** A discounted or complimentary consultation for new patients who book in December for a January appointment lowers the barrier to entry and starts the relationship before the calendar turns.

- **Loyalty patient priority booking.** Offer your existing loyalty program members priority access to January appointments before the books open publicly. This rewards loyalty and fills January with your most reliable patients.

- **Treatment plan pre-purchase.** For patients interested in a multi-step treatment plan (a full facial rejuvenation series, a body contouring package), offer a December pre-purchase incentive—a discount or bonus add-on for committing to the plan before January 1. Patients who pre-pay for treatment plans have dramatically higher completion rates.

- **January challenge or program.** A "30-Day Glow Challenge" or "New Year Skin Reset" program that starts in January can be marketed in December. Even if patients don't prepay, collecting email opt-ins for the program builds a targeted list you can activate with a booking offer in the first week of January.

## Key Metrics to Track

Running a Q4 campaign without tracking is guessing. These are the numbers that matter:

- **Gift card revenue:** Total dollar value of gift cards sold (by month and by denomination)
- **Gift card redemption rate:** How many sold gift cards get redeemed (and when)
- **New patient acquisition:** How many first-time patients booked via Q4 campaigns
- **Referral source for new patients:** Did they come via gift card, Instagram, Google, email, or a friend's recommendation?
- **Q4 revenue vs. prior year:** Year-over-year comparison tells you if your campaigns are actually working
- **January bookings made in December:** Your pipeline metric

Your practice management system (Zenoti, Boulevard, Jane App, Mindbody) should track most of these natively. Set up a simple weekly review with your practice manager from October through January to stay on top of what's working.

## Action Plan: What to Do This Week

September 1 is the right time to start. Here's your immediate action list:

**1. Audit your gift card situation.** Do you have physical cards? Do you offer digital? What's your purchase process? Fix any friction points before October.

**2. Build your Q4 email calendar.** Map out every email from now through December 31. Even rough subject lines are enough to start. Get it on paper.

**3. Set your package and promotion offers.** Decide on your Black Friday offer and your holiday package pricing before October so your team can start talking about it.

**4. Confirm your booking capacity.** How many appointments can you handle per week in November and December? Know your capacity before you promise availability you don't have.

**5. Schedule a team Q4 briefing.** Your front desk, providers, and marketing team need to be aligned on what's being promoted, when, and how to talk about it. A 30-minute meeting in late September sets this up.

The practices that look like they "had an amazing holiday season" planned for it months in advance. There's nothing more to it than that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When should I start promoting holiday gift cards?**
October 1 is the right launch date. Your most organized patients start holiday shopping in October, and being first to their inbox with an elegant gift option captures early buyers before competitors.

**Should I discount my services for Black Friday?**
Avoid discounting core services—it can cheapen your brand and condition patients to wait for sales. Instead, offer bonus value: a complimentary add-on, a gift card bonus, or priority booking access. Patients perceive this as generosity rather than a markdown.

**How do I promote gift cards without it feeling like a hard sell?**
Frame them in terms of the recipient's experience, not the purchase transaction. "Give someone you love the gift of confidence" lands differently than "Buy a gift card." Train your front desk to mention them warmly and naturally, not as a pitch.

**What if I don't have a loyalty program yet?**
Q4 is a great time to launch one. Even a simple "spend $X, earn $Y" program gives patients a reason to book multiple treatments and gives you a list segment to communicate with for the New Year campaign.

**How do I handle FSA/HSA questions from patients?**
Have a clear, accurate FAQ ready for your front desk team. Which of your services are FSA/HSA eligible depends on how your practice is structured and what the procedures are. Consult with your billing team or a healthcare compliance advisor to confirm what you can legitimately advertise as eligible, and train your team accordingly.
